How this publication works

Evidence before opinion, on every edition.

01

Evidence before opinion

Major claims link to a source, a dataset, or an explicit synthesis.

02

Respect the inside view

Public research cannot replace internal telemetry or the judgment of the product team.

03

Make uncertainty visible

Counter-evidence, limitations and rejected claims stay part of the published work.

04

Recommend experiments

Ideas are framed as bounded, testable hypotheses — not confident prescriptions.
